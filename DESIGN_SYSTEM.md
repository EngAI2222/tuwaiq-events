# DESIGN SYSTEM

## Core Philosophy
- **Vibe:** Luxury, Premium, Modern, Elegant, AI-Powered.
- **Direction:** RTL (Right-to-Left) natively optimized for the Arabic language.
- **Aesthetic:** High-contrast, rich colors, glassmorphism elements, subtle micro-animations.

## Typography
- **Primary Font:** Custom Arabic Font (e.g., Cairo, Tajawal, or IBM Plex Sans Arabic).
- **Headings:** Bold, clear, and elegant. High contrast.
- **Body:** Readable, sufficient line-height (1.6+).

## Color Palette
The color scheme is designed to convey luxury and trust:
- **Primary:** Deep Emerald Green or Midnight Blue (conveys trust and elegance).
- **Secondary / Accent:** Gold or Champagne (conveys luxury and premium quality).
- **Background:** 
  - Dark Mode: Rich charcoal/black with subtle gradients.
  - Light Mode: Off-white/pearl with soft shadows.
- **Text:** High contrast neutrals (Slate, Zinc).

## Components (Shadcn UI + Custom)
- **Buttons:** Fully rounded or slight radius, smooth hover transitions, glow effects for AI actions.
- **Cards:** Glassmorphism effect (backdrop-blur), subtle borders, soft shadows.
- **Forms:** Clean inputs, floating labels (optional), clear focus states using the primary accent color.
- **Animations:** Framer Motion for page transitions, scroll reveal animations, and interactive hover states.

## Responsive Strategy
- **Mobile First:** Designed to look exceptional on 320px - 430px screens.
- **Tablet:** 768px - 1024px optimizations for grids and navigation.
- **Desktop:** 1280px+ immersive experiences with wide imagery and multi-column layouts.
